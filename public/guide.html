The one-minute building

You want to see what this thing does.

Open the app. A sample cabin loads with a finished plan and a five-sheet drawing set (the tabs at the top: Model G-001 A-101…). Then, on the Build tab of the bottom tool rail:

  1. Tap MODEL — every wall becomes an exact solid; doors and windows cut real openings with headers and sills.
  2. Tap ROOF — a hip roof lands on the massing, valleys included.
  3. Tap DORMER, then tap the roof slope right where you want it — a 6-ft gabled dormer seats itself there.
  4. Tap DWGS — the whole documentation set regenerates: plans, four hidden-line elevations, a section, a roof plan, all on numbered sheets.

That loop — plan in, documented building out — is the reason this app exists. Everything else in this guide is detail.

The screen

You want to know what you're looking at.

Two habits worth forming on day one: press / to jump to the command line from anywhere, and press Esc to back out of whatever is happening.

Draw a plan

You want walls of your own instead of the sample.

  1. On the Draw tab, tap WALL. Tap where the wall starts, then where it ends. Keep tapping corners — walls heal as you draw: corners miter and T-junctions recut themselves.
  2. Type a length instead of hunting for it: mid-draw, type 12'6" and press Enter — the wall runs exactly that far along your direction. @8<45 means 8 feet at 45°.
  3. Turn on ORTHO (or F8) in the status bar to keep walls square while you learn.
  4. Close the loop back to the start. A closed loop of walls is a room-in-waiting.

Wall thickness lives in the WALL 6" chip above the rail — tap it to cycle 4″ / 6″ / 8″. Plain lines, rectangles and circles are the neighbouring tools (L, R, C from the keyboard).

Dimensions

Tap DIM, then the two points you're measuring, then where the dimension line should sit. Dimensions are associative — stretch or move the wall and the number follows.

Coming from AutoCAD? Type KEYMAP ACAD once and E erases, M moves, U undoes, X explodes, the way your hands expect. Full words (TRIM, COPY…) work in both maps.

Doors, windows, rooms

You want the plan to read like architecture, not line art.

  • Doors and windows live under SYMB on the Draw tab. Tap one, then tap the wall — it cuts the host wall itself. Selected, it grows grips: drag the square to resize the width (type 2'6" for exact), tap the diamond to flip the swing.
  • Rooms: type ROOMS to turn on live rooms — every closed wall loop becomes a named room with live square footage. Place a text label (TEXT) inside a loop to name it; the plan shows the name once and the area goes to the room schedule.
  • Fixtures (stove, bed, tub…) are in the same symbol drawer, with rotate and flip grips.

Make it 3D

You want the building, not just the plan.

The Build tab runs the pipeline in order, one tap each:

  • MODEL — walls become exact solids, openings cut through with headers and sills.
  • STACK — replicates the storey upward (type STACK 3 for three).
  • ROOF — hip roof at 6:12. Type ROOF GABLE 8 for a gable at 8:12. L- and T-shaped plans get real valleys.
  • DORMER — then tap the roof (in 3D or on the plan) where the dormer goes.
  • DWGS — regenerates the entire sheet set from the model.
  • QTO — drops a takeoff table: footprint, surface, volume per solid, measured straight from the meshes.

Freeform solids exist too: BOX 0 0 0 10 8 6 types a box by numbers; draw a closed shape and EXTRUDE 10 raises it; UNI and SUB on the 3D rail union and subtract (subtract is how you drill holes).

Touch the model

You want to grab the thing and change it.

Type 3D (or tap the globe in the top bar). Drag orbits, pinch or scroll zooms. Tap a solid to select it — then the left rail's mode buttons do the rest:

  • EDIT (E or Tab) — grab a corner, an edge, or the middle of a face and drag it. The mesh deforms live. This is how a gable becomes a saltbox: grab the ridge, pull it sideways.
  • PUSH (P) — drag any face along its own direction to thicken, extend, or carve.
  • ROT (R) — drag turns the solid in 15° steps.
  • MEAS (M) — tap two points for the true 3D distance.

Everything snaps to real geometry — corners lock to corners, edges to midpoints — and the readout names the lock (vertex, edge, face). Mid-drag, type a number and press Enter for an exact distance. Every change is one undo away.

Light & renders

You want it to look like a building, and a picture to show for it.

SUN JUN 21 14 40.7 — the real sun: month, day, hour, latitude. True shadows. MAT ROOF #7a3b2a — paint a solid, layer, or kind with a colour. RENDER 1600 — a print-resolution still, downloaded. Add PLACE to pin it on the drawing (it prints in the PDF). Add LEVEL to keep verticals vertical. VIEW SAVE HERO — remember this camera. VIEW HERO returns to it exactly. WALK 12 — a 12-second flythrough of your saved views, saved as video. TURNTABLE 8 — one full orbit of the model, saved as video.

Sheets & printing

You want paper — or the file a consultant asked for.

  • The tabs along the top are your sheets. G-001 is the cover; each room and view gets its own numbered page. Wheel or pinch zooms inside a sheet (the schedule cards open into full tables when you zoom in); double-tap refits.
  • Menu → Export PDF issues the set: 24×36 pages, title block, ISO lineweights, one page per sheet.
  • Menu → Export DXF is the file another CAD program opens — R2000, the AutoCAD Open path, with native hatches, dimensions and text. DWG is the same content in a DWG wrapper this app reopens.
  • STL / OBJ / GLB buttons in the 3D view export the model for printing or other 3D tools.
  • Your drawing autosaves on this device and never leaves it until you export. Menu → Save project writes the full .json file — that file always carries everything, including placed renders.

When you're stuck

You want out.

  • Esc cancels the current tool, drag, or 3D view. Ctrl-Z undoes anything — including MODEL, ROOF, and whole sheet regenerations.
  • The little messages at the bottom are honest: if a button needs something first, the toast says exactly what (“Nothing to roof: MODEL first”). Do the thing it names.
  • The order matters in Build: MODEL before ROOF before DORMER. Walls first, always.
  • If the app ever says it updated behind you, it reloads itself — you lose nothing; the drawing autosaves.
  • A fresh visit (or a private window) replays the built-in tour where the cabin builds itself.
